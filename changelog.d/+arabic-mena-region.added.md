Arabic language and Middle East coverage: `--region` (country codes/names such as `uae`, `jordan`, `saudi`, or groups `gcc`, `levant`, `maghreb`, `mena`) adds regional subreddits, a second web pass over national and pan-Arab news sites, and country defaults for Perplexity and Meta Ads; Arabic topics are detected, orthographically normalized for relevance and de-duplication, and routed to web search first. `LAST30DAYS_REGION` sets a default region.
